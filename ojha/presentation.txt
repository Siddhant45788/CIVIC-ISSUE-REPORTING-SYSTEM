Slide 3: Problem Statement
Title: Problem Statement
Content:
*   Inefficient and slow manual complaint registration processes.
*   Lack of a centralized system to track the status of complaints.
*   Difficulty in identifying the exact location of the issue.
*   Delayed response from authorities, leading to public dissatisfaction.
*   No accountability for unresolved issues.

Slide 4: Literature Survey
Title: Literature Survey
Content:
*   Existing systems often rely on manual intervention, which is time-consuming and prone to errors.
*   Some platforms lack real-time tracking and feedback mechanisms.
*   Mobile apps for civic issue reporting exist, but web-based accessibility is often limited.
*   Our proposed system integrates the best features from existing research and adds unique functionalities like automated severity analysis.

Slide 5: Proposed System
Title: Proposed System
Content:
*   A web-based portal for citizens to register complaints with photographic evidence.
*   GPS integration to automatically fetch the location of the issue.
*   A machine learning model to analyze the uploaded image and categorize the complaint (e.g., pothole, garbage, streetlight).
*   An admin dashboard for municipal authorities to view, assign, and track complaints.
*   Automated notifications to citizens and officials regarding the status of the complaint.

Slide 6: Methodology
Title: Methodology
Content:
1.  User Registration and Login: Citizens create an account or log in.
2.  Complaint Registration: Users fill a simple form, upload an image of the issue, and submit.
3.  Image Analysis: The ML model processes the image to identify the issue type and severity.
4.  Complaint Routing: The system automatically forwards the complaint to the relevant department.
5.  Resolution and Feedback: The assigned authority resolves the issue and updates the status. The citizen is notified and can provide feedback.
